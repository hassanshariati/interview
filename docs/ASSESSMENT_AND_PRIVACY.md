# Draft assessment and privacy policy — requires jurisdiction-specific legal review

- Assess **only demonstrated job-related skills** against versioned rubrics.
- Each competence assessment needs actual answer evidence, timestamps, evaluator identity, and room for correction.
- Distinguish unknown/not-assessed from incorrect responses.
- No automatic hiring/rejection; authorized reviewer makes final decision.
- Do **not** infer stress, emotions, honesty, illness, health or personal traits from voice, tremor, pitch, accent, disability or speech patterns.
- Silence, pauses, interruptions, poor microphone and connection quality are **not** negative scoring signals.
- Explain AI use and data processing to candidates before the session. Offer an accessible text alternative.
- Use opt-in recording where required; data minimization, retention/deletion schedule and access controls must be validated before production.
- Validate ASR on Persian/English accents, microphone conditions and accommodation needs.
- EU users: emotion recognition during recruitment is prohibited under the EU AI Act, and hiring-related candidate filtering/evaluation may be high-risk.
